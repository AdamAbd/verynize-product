# Third-party material and license scope

A project license can grant rights only to material its contributors are entitled to license. Dependencies, vendored fonts, icons, and other third-party assets retain their own licenses and notices. See `web/assets/fonts/LICENSE` and installed dependency license files; this document is not a complete dependency-license audit.

`design/wonder/` contains third-party application reference captures. `docs/wonder-harness-extract/` explicitly records extracted and verbatim vendor-served text. These materials are not granted a Verynize project license by this repository. Their presence does not establish permission to redistribute them. Review rights and remove or replace material without redistribution permission before describing a release as fully cleared. Deletion from the current tree does not remove earlier published Git history.

Independently written behavioral notes must remain distinguishable from copied vendor material. Wonder and other third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
